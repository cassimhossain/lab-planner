# Slide and Teacher-Guide Format: Robotics & AI Club

The format every presentation follows. It was written by studying the
club's existing decks (Foundation F-00 to F-04 and the Little Explorers,
Junior Makers, Young Innovators and Tech Leaders decks). Give this file to
whoever, or whatever, makes the next deck.

Decks are PDFs (16:9). See `rai/PHASE3_SLIDES_BRIEF.md` for the Robotics & AI
Curriculum decks and where to put them.

---

## 1. The look

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Page | 16:9, 960 × 540. Cream paper `#FFF8EE`. One soft colour blob top-right and one bottom-left. |
| Edge doodles | 3–4 tiny, faint line doodles round the edge of every slide: star, sparkle, squiggle, leaf, lightning, gear, bulb. Never over the content. |
| Top-left chip | `CODE · Title` in a pill, in the group/category colour. |
| Top-right tag | `LEARN`, `MAKE` or `WRAP UP` in an outlined pill. |
| Kicker | One short line above the title in **Caveat handwriting, orange-red `#E4572E`**: "Let's think...", "Objective 1 · Plant needs", "Build step 2 of 3", "Level up!". |
| Title | Heavy rounded display font, dark ink `#1B2333`, one line if possible. |
| Boxes | White with a **hand-drawn double outline** (two slightly wobbly lines) in one colour: green, blue, red, purple, amber or teal. Heading in the box colour, bold. A small line icon may sit before the heading. |
| Sticky notes | Yellow (facts, "Did you know?", "Fascinating fact", "At home") or blue (teacher notes, "Teacher: ..."). Text in **Caveat handwriting**, tilted about 1°. |
| Code | Dark rounded block, monospace, comments in grey. For young groups use **picture blocks** (coloured pills like ArduinoBlocks/mBlock), never typed code. |
| Footer | Left: `Robotics & AI Club · <Series or Group> · Curriculum by Qasim Mushtaq`. Right: `n / total`. |
| Colours | Category or group colour for the chip, tags and main accents. Red `#E0414B`, green `#2E9E5B`, blue `#2E6BE6`, purple `#7B3FC4`, amber `#F2A31B`, teal `#14967F`. |

## 2. Pictures: hand-drawn only

- **Every picture is a hand-drawn doodle**: flat colours, a dark marker
  outline (`#1B2333`), simple shapes and a slightly wobbly line. An Arduino is a teal
  board with black headers; a servo is a blue box with a white horn.
- **Never** use emoji, clip art, stock photos or images copied from the
  internet. The only photo-like image allowed is a YouTube thumbnail on the
  video slide.
- Draw every object for the lesson in this style (boards, sensors, motors,
  robots, laptops, scenes). Reuse drawings across decks so the set stays consistent.
- **Scenes:** a picture slide shows a small drawn scene in a rough frame,
  with short coloured labels under each object (e.g. *feels the soil →
  thinks → beep + light*), joined by hand-drawn arrows when it is a flow.

## 3. Slide order (every lesson)

**Title slide**
- Group/series line in handwriting (e.g. "Junior Makers · Class 6").
- A big two-colour title, then a one-line hook in blue.
- 3 skill tags (coloured pills).
- A large doodle on the right.
- A "Did you know?" sticky note at the bottom left.

**Part 1 · LEARN**
1. **Let's think... / Let's wonder...** A real-life question as the title, a red story box, three tilted guess buttons, a purple "Today: ..." strip at the bottom and a doodle on the right.
2. **Learning objectives**: "Today I can..." (young) or "By the end of this class I can..." (older). 2–4 numbered objectives, each with **Proof:**, meaning what the student shows to prove it.
3. **Objective 1 · ... · video**: "Video time!" Video thumbnail, title, channel, length, a "Watch for..." numbered list (3 questions), a QR code ("Scan to watch") and an "After the video:" task.
4. **Objective slides**, one or two per objective, kicker `Objective N · <short name>`: a green **"The idea"** box, an example box with a drawing, and a sticky note.
5. **Real world**: three boxes with a doodle, a name and one sentence each.
6. **Our machine / The plan**: a drawn scene of today's build (sense → think → act).
7. Optional: **Life skills** (safety), **Fair & private** (AI ethics), **Quick quiz**.

**Divider: Part 2 · Make it.** A full-colour slide: "Now let's put it to work! You know the ideas. Time to play, build and test. Use these slides while you work:" followed by a numbered list of the MAKE slides.

**Part 2 · MAKE**
8. **Class game**: an active game that acts out the big idea. "How to play" steps plus a "Big idea" note.
9. **Build it · Our kit (per group)**: the kit list with a drawing, plus a "Safety first" or "Check twice" note.
10. **Objective N · Build step 1 of 3** (then 2 of 3, 3 of 3): lettered steps (a, b, c), with a wiring scene, picture blocks or code on the right and a tip note.
11. **Experiment! / Test like an engineer**: a table with blanks to fill in.
12. **Level up!**: "Choose your challenge" (3–4 options), "Think like an engineer" (a question) and a **Boss level** note.
13. Optional: **Think about it / Debate**: two sides, with "Our rule".

**Part 3 · WRAP UP**
14. **Objective check**: "Can I really do it? Prove it." Rate yourself red, amber or green per objective.
15. **Show what you know**: "What did we learn?" ticks, an **Exit ticket** question, an **At home** note and **Next class:**.

Intro and board decks (like F-00 Welcome, F-01 Arduino, F-02 ESP32) add:
- **Robot profile slides**: kicker "Robot of the home · iRobot, USA · 2002", the robot's name as the title, a drawn scene with labels, "What it does", "SENSE / THINK / ACT", and a "Fascinating fact" note.
- **The 6 parts**: a 2 × 3 grid of boxes with icons and "X = the eyes, ears and skin".
- **History timeline**, **chip → module → board**, **specs**, **pin rules**, **side-by-side comparison**, **pin map**.
- **Coming up in the club**: a preview of the next builds.

## 4. Writing rules

- One idea per slide. At most about 45 words in a box; short sentences.
- Speak to the student: "you", "we", "Today I can...".
- Bold the key word in each box (`**word**`).
- Pakistani and everyday examples where possible (Sui gas, monsoon rain, cricket, school life).
- Every objective is taught (LEARN), practised (MAKE) and checked (WRAP UP).
- Safety notes are specific ("Teacher only", "Fingers clear of the flap").
- Three depths: **Starter** (play, picture blocks, the teacher builds), **Core** (the full lesson), **Advanced** (more maths, more code, more independence). Mark depth-only slides with `only: ["advanced"]`.

## 5. The teacher guide (one per lesson)

1. **At a glance**: length, type, grouping and concepts.
2. **Overview**: what students learn, and the one idea that must land.
3. **Learning outcomes.**
4. **Three depths**: what changes at Starter, Core and Advanced.
5. **Before the lesson**: the kit per group (★ for items not in the lab), simulators, and a prep checklist.
6. **Timing plan**: a table of minutes, phase and what happens; it must add up to the lesson length.
7. **Step by step**: for each phase, what to say and what to do.
8. **Common misconceptions**: what students might think, and how to help.
9. **Troubleshooting**: problem and fix.
10. **Assessment rubric**: Emerging, Secure and Extending, for 2–3 criteria.
11. **Answers** to the quick checks.
12. **Safety.**
